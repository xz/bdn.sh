> _This is a brief, quickly-written post covering the publication of top-level domains that were applied for during ICANN's 2026 New gTLD program. I'll be posting a more in-depth review soon!_

In the olden days of 1985, the only top-level domains (TLDs) were `.com`, `.net`, `.org`, `.edu`, `.gov`, `.mil`, `.int`, and the [pesky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/.arpa#History) `.arpa`. In the years to follow, new generic TLDs were occasionally added, leading to the realization that a formal TLD application process was needed. After the first New gTLD Application round concluded in 2012, no more TLD applications were considered...until now!

From April 30th to August 12th of 2026, organizations could pay [ICANN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICANN) a measly US$227,000 fee (reduced for some qualified groups) to apply for their very own top-level domain. And today, October 7th, the long-awaited mystery is over as ICANN [published](https://www.icann.org/en/announcements/details/icann-reveals-2026-round-applications-for-new-generic-top-level-domains-07-10-2026-en) the full list of TLDs that were applied for. (A mirror of the CSV file I downloaded on 2026-07-11 is available [here](https://cdn.bdn.sh/post-assets/2026-gtld-reveal/gtld-applications-2026-10-08T01-20-13-585Z.csv).)

There were a total of 1,615 applications, most being for generic TLDs anyone can register, but there were a surprising 333 brand applications for companies you might not expect, such as Bluesky's `.bsky`, Booking.com's `.yeah`, and Games Workshop's `.warhammer`.

I've read through many of the applications by now and wanted to share some interesting finds while I work on a full-length post covering more statistics of these applications. Enjoy!

## Potentially problematic strings
By getting to this stage of the application program, ICANN has tentatively okayed these names, but I feel like there may be community objection to some of these. I personally believe names like `.official` will cause nothing but problems. (How are you going to explain to someone who already struggles with using a computer that `google.official` isn't actually Google?)
- `.admin`
- `.auth`
- `.certified`
- `.lan`
- `.official`
- `.passkey`
- `.pdf`
- `.php`
- `.verified`
